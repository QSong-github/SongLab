# Research

The Song Lab develops multimodal AI methods for biomedical discovery and precision medicine. We integrate molecular, cellular, tissue, and patient data to understand disease mechanisms and therapeutic response.

## AI-driven drug discovery and therapeutic optimization

We develop graph-based and large language model-enhanced AI systems that integrate molecular structures, pharmacogenomics, and single-cell perturbation data. These methods predict drug response, investigate mechanisms of action and resistance, and prioritize therapeutic candidates. Our tools include DrugFormer, scDrugMap, DrugClaw, and CellPert.

## Spatial omics and histology integration

We design multimodal learning models that jointly analyze spatial transcriptomics and histopathology images. Our methods decode tissue microenvironments, identify spatially organized cellular niches, and recover molecular signals from sparse spatial data. Tools include SiGra, xSiGra, SpaIM, HECLIP, and GatorST.

## Mechanistic insights from multi-omics

We develop statistical and graph-based methods to integrate single-cell and bulk multi-omics data, revealing regulatory programs, cellular states, and pathway-level mechanisms of disease progression and therapeutic response. Our work spans single-cell knowledge transfer, regulatory integration, RNA structure, and alternative polyadenylation, with tools including scGCN, SMGR, VIRSE, and scDeepAPA.

## Multimodal patient modeling

We integrate longitudinal electronic health records, genomics, medical imaging, and real-world cohort data to support patient stratification, risk prediction, and personalized treatment strategies in cancer and neurodegenerative diseases. Projects include AlzCLIP and PHO-Agents, alongside studies of immunotherapy outcomes and adverse events.

## Selected research support

- **NIH MIRA, 1R35GM151089-01 (2023–2028).** Multi-modal insights of spatially distributed cells with associations of diseases and drug response. Principal Investigator: Qianqian Song.
- **Florida Department of Health, 26K08 (2026–2029).** Structure-aware modeling of alternative polyadenylation for cancer neoantigen discovery. Qianqian Song serves as Principal Investigator.
- **Southeastern Health AI Consortium Collaborative Grant (2026–2028).** Electronic health record large language model-based identification of individuals with likely incurable cancer to support depression treatment. Qianqian Song serves as Principal Investigator.
