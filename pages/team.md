# Team

## PI
Dr. Qianqian Song is an Associate Professor with tenure in the School of Applied and Creative Computing at Purdue University’s Polytechnic Institute, where she joined in August 2026. She directs the Purdue Polytechnic Digital Health Innovation Center (PDHIC). Previously, she held Assistant Professor appointments at the University of Florida and Wake Forest School of Medicine.

Her research combines graph AI, foundation models, large language models, and statistical methods to integrate single-cell and spatial omics, histology images, and clinical data for biomedical discovery and precision medicine.
