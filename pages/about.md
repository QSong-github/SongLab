# Qianqian Song, PhD

![Qianqian Song]({{ site.url }}/{{ site.picture }})

I am an Associate Professor with tenure in the School of Applied and Creative Computing at Purdue University’s Polytechnic Institute, where I joined in August 2026. I direct the Purdue Polytechnic Digital Health Innovation Center (PDHIC).

My lab develops AI-driven computational frameworks to integrate and interpret biomedical data across molecular, cellular, tissue, and patient scales. We combine graph AI, foundation models and large language models, multimodal learning, and statistical modeling with single-cell and spatial omics, histology images, electronic health records, and real-world clinical data. Our goal is to uncover disease mechanisms and therapeutic opportunities that advance precision medicine.

I have authored more than 100 peer-reviewed publications and developed more than 30 open-source computational tools and resources. My research is supported by an NIH Maximizing Investigators’ Research Award (MIRA) and other federal, state, foundation, and institutional awards.
